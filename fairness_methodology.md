# Fairness & Bias Audit Methodology

## Group Definition
- Method: Use only authorized and appropriately consented group attributes.
- Decision / limitation: Privacy, consent and governance review required.

## Reference Group
- Method: Select the reference group before interpreting disparity.
- Decision / limitation: Prevents changing the comparison after observing results.

## Outcome Definition
- Method: Define the business outcome using a production event and fixed reporting window.
- Decision / limitation: Ensures groups are compared on the same outcome.

## Disparity Measurement
- Method: Calculate both absolute and relative outcome differences.
- Decision / limitation: Separates magnitude from relative disparity.

## Uncertainty
- Method: Report confidence intervals or other uncertainty estimates when production observations are available.
- Decision / limitation: Avoid treating small-sample differences as reliable effects.

## Causal Limitation
- Method: Observed disparity is not treated as proof of discrimination or causal mechanism.
- Decision / limitation: Requires further investigation or controlled testing.

## Consent & Proxies
- Method: Do not infer protected attributes from unrelated behavioural or demographic fields.
- Decision / limitation: Protects against unsupported proxy-based conclusions.
