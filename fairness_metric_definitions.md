# Fairness Metric Definitions

## Selection Rate
- Definition: Proportion of eligible users receiving the measured positive outcome.
- Raw source: Production outcome and eligibility events
- Business decision: Identify differences in outcome rates across groups.

## Outcome Rate
- Definition: Proportion of users in each group reaching the defined business outcome.
- Raw source: Production outcome events
- Business decision: Monitor outcome disparities.

## Outcome Rate Ratio
- Definition: Group outcome rate divided by the reference-group outcome rate.
- Raw source: Production outcome observations
- Business decision: Identify relative disparity requiring investigation.

## Absolute Outcome Gap
- Definition: Difference between a group's outcome rate and the reference-group outcome rate.
- Raw source: Production outcome observations
- Business decision: Quantify the absolute size of observed disparity.
